## Is the probe's REFERENCE MODEL the harness's actual reference? Three checks.

The `(c)` disposition of `06f7d2c8` and `26021804` rests entirely on one
modelling claim -- that the harness's reference chain is unbounded internally and
gates ONCE, on the final message, at the generated bridge's export. If that were
wrong the whole finding would be wrong, so it is checked against the instrument
three ways rather than asserted.

1. THE MODEL PREDICTS THE FIRST TAKE'S FAILING SET, EXACTLY. From the two
   lengths it predicts red at capacities [L_callee, L_final) = [16, 30) and green
   everywhere else. The first dispatch measured, with the emitted test's own diff
   cap raised so every index was recorded, cases 1140..1153 of 1387 -- fourteen,
   contiguous, and nothing outside them
   (`harness.first_take.all_14_cases.json`). Predicted set == measured set, and
   the prediction was written down before the set was read.

2. THE MODEL PREDICTS WHICH CASES A GREEN CORPUS MAY CONTAIN, AND IT IS GREEN.
   The second dispatch removed exactly the fourteen the model names -- no more,
   no fewer -- and re-ran: `harness/AeroDynTorque.json`, 1373 checked, 0 failed,
   0 inadmissible. A model that had the window wrong by one case in either
   direction would have left a red case in the corpus or dropped a green one, and
   `vit_mutate.py` refuses to score against a red baseline, so the mutation sweep
   is a second consumer of the same prediction.

3. THE SHIPPED PROGRAM PRINTS THE THREE GATES. The post-integration run's own
   stderr, per case, at a stated capacity of 14:

       VIT: interp1d: ErrVar%ErrMsg needs 16 bytes, the staging buffer holds 14;
            the assignment is refused
       VIT: AeroDynTorque: ErrVar%ErrMsg needs 21 bytes, the staging buffer
            holds 14; the assignment is refused
       VIT bridge: ErrVar%ErrMsg assignment of 30 bytes exceeds the 14-byte
            buffer the caller supplied; left unchanged

   Two gates inside the translation and one at the bridge, on one buffer, at
   three different lengths -- which is the composition the model encodes. BELOW
   the window all three refuse and the case agrees, which is why the stated hole
   is [16, 29] and not [0, 29].

A fourth, weaker check is already committed: `mutation.survivors_on_full_corpus.txt`
ran all four survivors over the FULL 1387-case corpus and found the failing case
SET of these two IDENTICAL to the unmutated baseline's -- they add no failure
anywhere in it. That is the corpus saying what the probe says about the domain.
