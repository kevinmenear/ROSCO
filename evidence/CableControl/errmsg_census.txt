THE CAUSE IS ONE FACT AND IT IS THE BASE DRAW, NOT THE LADDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Every rung of every ladder that could supply one of the three inputs is taken
`with every other input at its base draw` -- R13's own coverage line says so --
and this unit's base draw is:

  ErrVar_aviFAIL   [(-2147483648, 2), (-1000000000, 2), (-999999999, 2), (-100000000, 2), (-99999999, 2), (-10000000, 2)] ...
  CntrPar_CC_Mode  [(-2147483648, 2), (-1000000000, 2), (-999999999, 2), (-100000000, 2), (-99999999, 2), (-10000000, 2)] ...

  case 0:  aviFAIL = 300   CC_Mode = -300

300 and -300 are MIDPOINTS of the +/-1e3 default range, which is unit #47's
finding met a second time: `_base` puts a selector at the midpoint of its
range and a midpoint is not a mode. So R13's 256 capacity cases and R6's
character-length rungs all sit at `aviFAIL = 300`, where `IF (ErrVar%aviFAIL
< 0)` is false and neither helper is entered at all -- which is why the
capacity ladder and the arm that reads the capacity are DISJOINT.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE THEM, AND WHY IT WAS NOT DONE HERE
---------------------------------------------------

TWO of the three need only that `aviFAIL` be negative WHERE R13's capacity ladder
and R6's character-length ladder already run, and a `lo`/`hi` pin on
`ErrVar_aviFAIL` moving its BASE DRAW would do that. The THIRD, `1a26a963`, needs
`n_ErrMsg <= 0` -- the field's LENGTH -- and NO judgement kind in
`harness/ranges.toml` can state it: the file has exactly six (`lo`/`hi`,
`values`, `text`, `same_as`, `no_oracle`, `ordered_only`/`nondecreasing_only`,
`staging_capacity_excludes`), `text` pins a string's CONTENT in every case rather
than adding a length to the ladder, and `_baseline_state` skips every `char[]`
parameter outright. The enumeration is in DECISIONS.md as a table.

All three need a CHARACTER parameter's LENGTH or a staging CAPACITY crossed
with the `aviFAIL` knob. `harness/baseline.CableControl.json` cannot do it:
`_baseline_state` deliberately skips every `char[]` parameter
(harness/generate.py:1138), and `harness/ranges.toml` has no entry kind that
states a character length. The two levers that exist are a range pin moving
`aviFAIL`'s and `CC_Mode`'s BASE DRAW onto a live arm (`lo`/`hi`), and making
them R2 FLAGS (`values = [...]`), which is unit #47's fix and multiplies the
corpus. Either is a change to the generated corpus that every later artifact
of this unit would have to be re-taken over, and this dispatch stopped at the
measurement rather than starting a re-take it could not finish (RUNBOOK step
2). THIS IS THE THIRD UNIT AT THE SAME WALL: unit #43 recorded it as
'the ErrMsg helpers' three boundaries ... no state in this file can move
them', unit #48 from the other side, and this unit measures the reason.
