WHAT THE FIRST ONE SAYS, AND IT AGREES WITH COVERAGE TO THE SCENARIO.
`coverage/line_coverage.json` puts 3,998 hits on clean Controllers.f90:912 --
the first step constant -- under scenario 7, 3,998 under scenario 27 and ZERO
under scenario 3. The red test moves exactly scenarios 7 and 27, and 3,999 of
each scenario's 24,000 timesteps: the steps after t = 500, which is the guard.

WHY `cc_actuated_dl` MOVES ON 3,287 AND `cc_actuated_l` ON 3,999. The DL
channel is the SecLPFilter_Vel output, which decays back toward the new
constant; the L channel is the PIController integral of it, which never
forgets. 712 of the DL samples land on the same REAL(4) as before and the
integral's do not.

THE GATE'S RESOLUTION ON THIS UNIT IS SINGLE PRECISION, AND THE SECOND RED
TEST IS WHERE THAT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSERTED. Everything this unit
sends to a gate channel goes through `avrSWAP`, which is `REAL(ReKi) ::
avrSWAP(*)` -- REAL(4). PI moved in its twelfth significant digit is a
relative change of about 3e-12, ~5 orders below the ~1.2e-7 spacing of
binary32, so it moves 2 values in 5,252,000: one sample in each of the two
live scenarios that happened to straddle a rounding boundary. It IS red and it
IS revert-verified, and it is the wrong instrument to certify this unit with --
which is why the step constant, one part in 1,451, is the one committed as
`gate/CableControl.redtest.json`. A unit whose outputs narrow to REAL(4) needs
a coarser perturbation than unit #47's or unit #48's, both of which reached
REAL(8) channels.

Scenario 3's `cc_actuated_l` moves on 15,999 of its 16,000 samples. THE SITE IS
FULLY OBSERVABLE. What annihilates the two real perturbations is that scenario
3 runs to t = 400 and every writer of `LocalVar%CC_DesiredL` in this unit is
behind `IF (LocalVar%Time > 500)`, so the filter's input is exactly 0.0 for the
whole run: `1/a2 * (b2*0 + b1*0 + b0*0 - a1*0 - a0*0)` is 0.0 for every value
of the six coefficients, the integrator integrates 0.0, and both avrSWAP slots
are 0.0 whatever PI or the step constants hold.

This is the same shape as unit #47's scenario 8 (`AWC_amp` at 0.0) and unit
#48's scenario 17 (`WE_Gamma` at 0.0), and it is the third instance in three
units. The difference here is that the zero is not a configured GAIN -- it is
the initial value of a state the scenario's own run length never lets the unit
write. Closing it needs a scenario that reaches t > 500 with CC_Mode = 1, which
scenarios 7 and 27 already are; scenario 3 adds nothing this unit can be
measured on and the gate says so.
