WHAT THE 20,592 RED CONFIGURATIONS ARE, STATED SO THEY ARE NOT MISREAD
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The sweep crosses `n` from -2 to 32 against `cap` from 0 to 64 independently, so
it includes 20,592 configurations with `n > cap` -- a stated length longer than
the buffer that holds it. THE EMITTED TEST CANNOT PRODUCE THOSE: it sizes the
staging vector as `max(n,0) + headroom` (cablecontrol_test.cpp:1296), so `n <=
cap` always, and the census confirms it on the scored corpus (`cap - need` is
4078 or 4079 in all 223 prefix cases). They are swept anyway because excluding
them would make the probe's own domain a claim rather than a measurement, and
because the three survivors' kill sets are entirely OUTSIDE them -- which is what
`separates == kills` says.

WHAT THE PROBE DOES NOT MODEL
-----------------------------

The `CC_Mode == 2` path, on which `interp1d` writes `ErrVar%ErrMsg` through its
own bridge before this unit prefixes it. That is unit #48's non-compositional
staging window, and it is not modelled because it does not arise on the corpus:
the harness is GREEN at 3354 of 3354, and R13's capacity block runs at
`CC_Mode = -300` where the arm cannot execute. A corpus rule that put a negative
`aviFAIL` under the R13 ladder WITHOUT also putting `CC_Mode = 2` there keeps it
that way; one that does both would need the `staging_capacity_excludes` hole unit
#48 states. Written down because it is the trap the next dispatch would fall in.
