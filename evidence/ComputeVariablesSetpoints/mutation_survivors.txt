DECLARING WITHOUT THE MAP WOULD BE A FALSE EQUIVALENCE, and P12 fails a unit
outright for one, so they stay open. What blocks the map is a concrete,
reproducible thing rather than a judgement: `cppmutate._mid(unit, operator,
before, after, occurrence)` recomputed by hand does NOT reproduce any of the
sweep's ids -- `_mid('ComputeVariablesSetpoints','compare_op','<','<=',k)`
gives b512bf09, b424866a, 38d46d45 ... for k = 0, 1, 2 while the sweep's is
f977278e -- so `vit_mutate.py` is not deriving its ids from
`cppmutate.mutants()` in the way the function's own signature suggests.
Reconciling those two is one afternoon and it closes at least two mutants
here and, being an instrument fix, on every later unit as well.
