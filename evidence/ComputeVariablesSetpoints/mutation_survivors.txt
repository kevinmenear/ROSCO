FOUR SWEEPS, AND THE SCORE IS A MEASUREMENT OF THE CORPUS RATHER THAN OF THE
TRANSLATION AT EVERY STEP:

    0.7161   the corpus as generated, nothing declared
    0.7342   + the two dead-store equivalences

    0.8354   + `CntrPar_VS_RefSpd` UNPINNED again        <- committed

The last step is the one worth reading: pinning `VS_RefSpd` to the shipped
122.90967 makes `saturate`'s upper bound observable and DESTROYS both Region-3
`VS_FBP` lookups, because R6's RELATIONAL PAIR block requires both sides of
`VS_RefSpd_TSR > VS_RefSpd` to be varied and disappears when either is pinned.
Two mutants either way; the version that keeps the two ARMS is committed.

TWO DECLARED EQUIVALENT, AND THE DECLARATION HAS ITS CONTROL INSIDE THE SWEEP.

  312f3565  drop_factor  'VS_RefSpd_TSR * PRC_R_Speed' -> 'VS_RefSpd_TSR'
  f8e46a25  arith_op     'VS_RefSpd_TSR * PRC_R_Speed' -> 'VS_RefSpd_TSR / PRC_R_Speed'

Both sit on the reference's own DEAD STORE, clean source
`ControllerBlocks.f90:148`:

    LocalVar%VS_RefSpd = LocalVar%VS_RefSpd_TSR * LocalVar%PRC_R_Speed
    LocalVar%VS_RefSpd = LPFilter(LocalVar%VS_RefSpd_TSR, ...)

The second statement OVERWRITES the first and reads `VS_RefSpd_TSR`, not
`VS_RefSpd`; there is no statement between them and `LPFilter`'s seven actual
arguments do not include `VS_RefSpd`. No admissible input can make either
mutant change an output: a claim about the PROGRAMS.

**THE CONTROL IS THAT THE SAME PRODUCT ONE SCREEN DOWN IS NOT DECLARED.**
`CntrPar%VS_RefSpd * LocalVar%PRC_R_Speed` is `saturate`'s upper bound at
`:162` and it IS read, so `09894fe5` and `be35d649` stay OPEN below. Two
identical edits to two identical expressions, one pair declared and one pair
not, and the difference is dataflow rather than judgement.

THIRTEEN OPEN. THREE GROUPS, AND NONE OF THEM IS A DEFECT CLAIM.

--- (a) `saturate`'s upper bound, ANNIHILATED BY THE UNIT'S OWN LAST STATEMENT

  09894fe5  drop_factor  'CntrPar->VS_RefSpd * PRC_R_Speed' -> 'CntrPar->VS_RefSpd'
  be35d649  arith_op     the same expression, '*' -> '/'

`saturate(x, VS_MinOMSpd, VS_RefSpd*PRC_R_Speed)` returns at most the bound,
and the unit's LAST statement is `MAX(VS_RefSpd, VS_MinOMSpd)` -- so whenever
the bound is below `VS_MinOMSpd` the clamp lifts the answer back and the bound
cannot be read. With `VS_RefSpd` free its base draw is -600 and the bound is
-480, below the pinned `VS_MinOMSpd` of 34.64. Counted from the other side:
`negate_cond` on that arm's own `if` moved 35 of 25,398 cases on the first
corpus.

TWO REPAIRS WERE PRICED AND BOTH COST MORE THAN THEY BUY:
  * pin `CntrPar_VS_RefSpd = 122.90967` -- kills these two, LOSES both
    Region-3 `VS_FBP` arms (0.797 against 0.835), because the relational pair
    block needs both sides varied;
  * pin `LocalVar_PRC_R_Speed = -0.8` so the bound is positive at the base
    draw -- measured, and the survivor set is IDENTICAL. It bought nothing, so
    the physical +0.8 is committed.

--- (b) three `compare_op` boundary mutants, each needing an exact tie

  1c85d6d7  '>'  -> '>='
  f977278e  '<'  -> '<='
  f647c7b4  '>'  -> '>='

The `<` is `LocalVar%SS_DelOmegaF < 0`, whose two arms are
`PC_RefSpd_PRC - SS_DelOmegaF` and `PC_RefSpd_PRC`. At `SS_DelOmegaF == 0`
those agree for every finite value AND for a NaN, because `x - 0.0 == x`
including the sign of the zero. **The only input that separates them is
`SS_DelOmegaF == -0.0` with `PC_RefSpd_PRC == -0.0`**, where the mutated arm
computes `-0.0 - (-0.0) = +0.0`. R6 carries a negative-zero rung per scalar and
this needs TWO parameters at negative zero simultaneously; the generator moves
one at a time. NOT declared equivalent, because it is not -- it is a corpus
that cannot reach a real difference.

--- (c) eight `const_tweak` survivors, and the id-to-site map is what is missing

  0c23917a '0'->'1'   9b32d4a5 '1'->'2'   c3c9ad10 '0'->'1'   860bdd9e '0'->'1'
  c303585e '0.0'->'1.0'   30056504 '1'->'2'   b1377f87 '0'->'1'   642f565d '0.0'->'1.0'

TWO OF THESE ARE ALMOST CERTAINLY EQUIVALENT AND ARE DELIBERATELY NOT DECLARED.
This translation has four `0.0` literals: the `InitialValue` argument of each of
the two `lpfilter_c` calls, and the second argument of each
`fortran_max(x, 0.0)`. `LPFilter`'s eighth dummy is OPTIONAL and the
`has_InitialValue` flag beside it is the literal `0`, so the bridge never reads
the value and no input can make those two mutants change an output.

**DECLARING WITHOUT THE MAP WOULD BE A FALSE EQUIVALENCE, AND P12 FAILS A UNIT
OUTRIGHT FOR ONE.** What blocks the map is concrete and reproducible:
`cppmutate._mid(unit, operator, before, after, occurrence)` recomputed by hand
does NOT reproduce any of the sweep's ids --
`_mid('ComputeVariablesSetpoints','compare_op','<','<=',k)` gives `b512bf09`,
`b424866a`, `38d46d45` … for k = 0, 1, 2 while the sweep's is `f977278e` -- so
`vit_mutate.py` is not deriving its ids the way that function's signature
suggests. A PARTIAL workaround was used here and is worth writing down: zip the
ordered `[n/81] … SURVIVED` lines from the sweep's own stdout against the
ordered `SURVIVOR <id>` summary, after removing the declared-equivalent lines.
It recovers every survivor whose printed text names an expression; it does not
recover the `const_tweak` family, whose lines print only the literal.
Reconciling the two is an instrument fix that pays on every later unit.

WHAT 0.8354 DOES NOT COVER, STATED PLAINLY:

  * `saturate`'s upper bound -- and the GATE cannot see it either (35 of
    25,398 for its own predicate). No instrument in this campaign currently
    discriminates that statement.
  * three arm boundaries that need an exact IEEE tie, one of them needing two
    negative zeros at once.
  * two `lpfilter_c` arguments that are provably unread, undeclared only
    because the tool cannot tell them from two that are not.
