THE THREE NON-ZEROS PARTITION THE FILE EXACTLY, which is what makes them a
measurement rather than three numbers that happen to be non-zero.

  24        = one heading record per file, and the heading is the only record
              carrying a truncated literal.
  407,976   = 408,072 - 96, and 96 is 4 records per file that hold no
              ES-formatted number: the 'Generated on' record, the two heading
              records, and the empty trailing record every text file ends with.
  21,792    = the records holding at least one DebugOutData value whose
              magnitude is below 1E-99. That is 5.3% of the file and it is a
              live arm.

TWO ZEROS, AND THEY ARE BLINDNESS RATHER THAN BREAKAGE -- the control is on the
SAME BUILD and the same sweep, since the three rows above moved 24, 407,976 and
21,792 through the identical mechanism.

  THE UPPER CLAMP IS DEAD. No DebugOutData value in any of the 27 scenarios
  exceeds 1E+99, so `IF (ABS(x) > 1E+99) x = 1E+99` never fires. Its sibling one
  line up fires on 21,792 records. This is the shape unit #28 recorded for
  wrap_360: one live arm and one dead one, in a pair, where the per-unit number
  reports only the live one.

  THE ENTIRE .RO.dbg2 AND .RO.dbg3 PATHS ARE DEAD, and the cause is one line of
  configuration rather than an unreached branch. All 14 Examples/DISCON*.IN set
  `LoggingLevel = 1`, so `IF (CntrPar%LoggingLevel > 1)` and `> 2` are REACHED
  in every scenario and FALSE in every scenario. Committed coverage says the
  same thing from the other side: ROSCO_IO.f90:1108 and :1116 (the guards) have
  hits in 23 scenarios; :1109, :1118, :1183 and :1187 (the bodies) have none.
  What is invisible is a third of the unit -- the 159 LocalVarOutData
  assignments and their clamps, the 159-name heading table, the avrIndices
  construction with its two AddToList loops, and the vector-subscripted
  avrSWAP(avrIndices) write.

  Both are configuration facts about the shipped inputs, not defects in the
  harness (unit #27's distinction). Neither is repairable without changing what
