The two arms therefore have DISJOINT scenario sets, which is unit #40's rule
that two arms need two red tests. All three runs below are in this unit's own
translation unit, all three assert `replacements: 1`, and all three are
revert-verified by `scripts/gate.py` itself -- it reverts, rebuilds and re-runs
the whole gate in the same process, and every one came back at 0 of 5,252,000.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. THE UNIT'S ANSWER, OFFSET.   gate/FloatingFeedback.redtest.json

     return FloatingFeedback_result;  ->  return FloatingFeedback_result + 0.01;

     moved 404,454 of 5,252,000    scenarios 3, 7, 19, 27    revert 0

     channels: bld_pitch, bld_pitch_2, bld_pitch_3, gen_power, gen_speed,
     gen_torque -- 24 channel/scenario pairs.

     ADDITIVE AND NOT A SCALE, on unit #50's finding. `Fl_PitCom` is a pitch
     contribution and nothing in this campaign guarantees it is non-zero in any
     given scenario; a scale factor cannot be red-tested through an exact zero,
     and one additive run settles it without a separating probe afterwards.
     0.01 rad is ~0.57 deg, four orders above the REAL(4) resolution of the
     `avrSWAP(42..44)` slots the pitch channels come from (unit #49's rule about
     picking the perturbation from the CHANNEL's width).

     This is the coarse test and it is the one committed as `.redtest.json`.

  THE CONTROL THAT MAKES THE ATTRIBUTION A MEASUREMENT AND NOT AN INFERENCE:

      223,222  (arm 1, scenarios 3 and 7)
    + 181,232  (arm 2, scenarios 19 and 27)
    = 404,454  (the shared return, scenarios 3, 7, 19, 27)

  EXACTLY. The two arm-scoped perturbations partition the shared one's total to
  the value, and their scenario sets are disjoint and their union is the shared
  one's. So the arms really are mutually exclusive at run time, each arm's own
  arithmetic is separately observable through the gate, and neither arm is
  riding on the other's scenarios. `mismatched_channels` in each artifact names
  the scenarios, so this is READ rather than asserted (unit #47's rule).

  NOTHING HERE MOVED ZERO. This is the first unit in five without an
  annihilated-by-an-exact-zero finding -- #47's `AWC_amp`, #48's `WE_Gamma`,
  #49's unwritten `CC_DesiredL` and #50's 1-DOF `rootMOOPF` each had one. The
  reason is structural rather than lucky: this unit's answer is ADDED to
  `LocalVar%PC_PitComT`, which is a live pitch command in every scenario that
  reaches the call, and the perturbations are additive at the point of the sum.

  WHAT THE GATE STILL CANNOT SEE HERE. `LocalVar%Kp_Float` reaches no channel
  of its own -- it is consumed by the two arms and by nothing else in ROSCO --
  and `objInst%instPI` reaches none either. A defect that got the gain schedule
  or the instance counter wrong is visible to the gate ONLY through the product
  it forms, and to `piP` not at all. The differential harness and the mutation
  sweep are the instruments that constrain those; the gate constrains the
  answer.
