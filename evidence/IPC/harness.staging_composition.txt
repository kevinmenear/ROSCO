WHY THE CASE HAS NO ORACLE INSIDE THE WINDOW. The reference chain is Fortran
throughout with a reallocating ErrMsg and ONE capacity gate -- the generated
bridge's export, on the final 35 bytes. The translation chain gates FIVE
times: once in each sigma_c bridge write-back and once in assign_errmsg.
Inside the window the reference holds the entry value and the translation
holds a partial composition, and the reference's OWN answer -- 35 bytes of
'IPC:sigma:sigma:sigma:sigma:abyz{9:' -- is a value NEITHER side can carry.
A case whose oracle records something upstream ROSCO never produces is not a
case this comparison can adjudicate (P7). Unit #48 measured the same shape at
two gates; this is the same shape at five.
