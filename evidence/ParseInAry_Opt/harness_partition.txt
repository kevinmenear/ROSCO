ParseInAry_Opt -- the differential harness partitioned by (alloc_Ary on entry, arm)
MEASURED ON THE SECOND DISPATCH'S CORPUS (15,504 cases), after the two
base-draw corrections in harness/ranges.toml. The first dispatch's table, on
the 13,674-case corpus, is in the git history of this file.

HOW: one fprintf in the generated test and a rebuild WITHOUT regenerating the
corpus -- unit #47's probe. It reads THREE things and all three are properties
of (corpus x reference):
  alloc_Ary  the descriptor flag the CASE supplied, read before either call
  entry      ErrVar%aviFAIL as the CASE supplied it, snapshotted before both
             calls because both of them overwrite it
  arm        what the REFERENCE returned: its aviFAIL, whether its ErrMsg
             length moved, and byte 16 of the message it left
Nothing on the translation's side is read, so the table is identical under
every mutant and every stub -- which is what makes a mutation score over it a
measurement.  `error, msg refused` is the arm where the reference DID set
aviFAIL = -1 and the bridge would not fit the message in the staging buffer;
that is a real cell here rather than a corner, because R13 sweeps the
capacity from below the message length to above it.

THE STATED EXCLUSION -- alloc_Ary == 0 AND entry aviFAIL >= 0 AND the
reference returned aviFAIL < 0 -- holds on 1283 of 15504 cases (8.3%).
On the first dispatch's corpus it held on 4,067 of 13,674 (29.7%), so the
corrected base draw did not widen the region the harness cannot judge: it
SHRANK it to a quarter of its share, because `alloc_Ary == 1` cases have a
perfectly defined Ary and the corrected draw puts more of the corpus there.
The one-relation form (dropping the entry-status half) excludes 1467,
184 too many -- again the cases that arrived already failed.

AND THE CHECK ON THE EXCLUSION IS THE GREEN ITSELF: 0 failing cases means no
case OUTSIDE the condition disagrees on Ary. That check is not free here --
see DECISIONS.md on the corpus variant that was measured and NOT taken,
in which exactly one case outside the condition did.
