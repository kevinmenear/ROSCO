INSTRUMENT FOR THEM.** Each reads `rec[2048]`, one past the end of the

`-fsanitize=address,undefined` all eleven abort with a heap-buffer-overflow, and
the shipped translation runs the same 131,298 records clean.

**THE DISTINGUISHING RECORD IS ALMOST NOTHING: `0` FOLLOWED BY BLANKS TO COLUMN
2048.** Six of the eleven die on exactly that; the others on `0a`, `0 +` and
` 3`. The mutation sweep already runs `--sanitize`, so the reason they survive
the sweep is not the instrument -- it is that no case in the 17,520 hands the
parser a record with nothing to the right of its last value.

**AND THAT IS A FACT ABOUT HOW A RECORD REACHES THE PARSER, NOT ABOUT THE
CORPUS'S ALPHABET.** The unit only reads a line `FindLine` matched, and
`FindLine` matches on word `AryLen + 1` -- so in every record that reaches the
READ there is a non-blank KEY to the RIGHT of the last value, and every
separator scan stops on it, thousands of columns short of the boundary.
`_RECORD_TAILS` (loop 59aa876) does not reach this: its four forms end the
record inside a VALUE, which is the boundary `parse_int`'s own scan meets and
not the one `eat_separator` meets.

For the corpus to hand the parser a record ending in blanks, `FindLine` would
have to match a line whose word `AryLen + 1` is blank -- which needs an
all-blank `ParamName`, and that is the same input shape as the THIRD
undefined-`Ary` path this unit escalated in DECISIONS.md. **Escalated, not
declared:** it is a corpus lever with a known cost, and pricing it is the next
