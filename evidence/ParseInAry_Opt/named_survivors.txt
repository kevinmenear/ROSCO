WHAT THE CORPUS CANNOT DO, measured rather than asserted. The differential
harness reaches the parser at all only where `FindLine` matches, and `FindLine`
matches a WORD of a file line against `ParamName`. Two independently drawn
strings never coincide, so the only matches the corpus produces are R14's
deliberate plant and the all-blank coincidence. R14's alphabet is `aA`, `bB`,
`cC` -- LETTERS. So every record the parser is ever handed either is blank or
begins with a letter, and it fails at the first character, before any sign is
read. The no-read stub measures the whole region at 146 cases of 15,504.

This dispatch made R14 plant at words 1, 2 AND 3 rather than word 1 alone (the
`UnEc` entry below), which is why the region grew from 103 of 13,674. It does
not put a DIGIT in front of a matched word, and it cannot: the plant alphabet
is `generate.py`'s, shared by every unit in the campaign. Unit #54 escalated
exactly this to the Driver -- "every unit in the `Parse*_Opt` family is a parser
behind that search, so every one will reach its parser in ~1% of its cases
unless the rule plants more" -- and it is the Driver's to weigh.

The `UnEc` correction is the part that WAS in this unit's authority:

  UnEc = { lo = 0, hi = 0, values = [0] }

R14 sets EVERY free scalar integer to `k-1` and to `k` in the same case and
drops the whole shape when any one of them is outside its stated range. `UnEc`
is pinned to exactly 0 by this unit's own entry, so `k = 2` and `k = 3` needed
`UnEc` at 1, 2 or 3 and were dropped -- a pin on a parameter the rule is not
about deleted two thirds of the rule, and the artifact said so
(`NOT reached ['2:plant', '3:plant']`) for a whole dispatch before anyone read
it. Stating `values` as well as the bounds takes `UnEc` out of `plant_ints`
while pinning it at the same single value; the regenerated corpus carries
`UnEc = 0` in all 15,504 cases, asserted, and R14 now reports
`Reached ['1:plant', '2:plant', '3:plant']`.

Both widen a record-bound check so that at `p == len` the mutant reads
`rec[len]`. `rec` is `Line.data()` and `Line` is `std::vector<char>(2048)`
(translation line 473), so `rec[len]` is ONE PAST THE END of the allocation.
That is undefined behaviour, not a defined read of a blank -- the vector has no
guaranteed element there, unlike a `std::string`, whose `data()[size()]` is a
defined NUL. So this is NOT case (a): there is no argument that the two
programs agree on every admissible input, only an observation that on this
allocator the byte that happens to follow is another blank and the answer does
not change.

NEITHER INSTRUMENT REACHES THEM, and that is measured on both:

  differential harness   survived
  parser replay          unreached, 0 of 113 records

They are two of the nineteen READ-region survivors that nothing reaches, of
which fifteen are this same shape (`p < len` -> `p <= len` and `rec[p]` ->
`rec[p + 1]` at record bounds). What would kill them is not a case -- it is a
BUILD: ASan or `_GLIBCXX_DEBUG`, under which a one-past-the-end read faults
instead of returning a blank. That is unit #31's open amendment. This unit adds
only that these two, and the other seventeen, belong to it -- measured, not
assumed.
