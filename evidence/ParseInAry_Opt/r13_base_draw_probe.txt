`assign_errmsg` refuses when `s.size() > cap`; the Fortran bridge writes when
`LEN(msg) <= cap`. The two agree everywhere EXCEPT at `s.size() == cap`, so a
corpus with zero such cases cannot tell `>` from `>=`. That is survivor
66860b6f, and it is a corpus gap (b), not an equivalence (a).
