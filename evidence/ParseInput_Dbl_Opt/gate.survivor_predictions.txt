   The exponent's SIGN. `IF (has_exp)` then `IF (p < len && (rec[p] == '+' ||

   4.74029e+04, VS_MinTq 0.00000e+00 -- are read by THIS unit.

   `perturbation_broke_scenarios` non-empty, because a parse failure sets
   aviFAIL = -1 and ROSCO reports a fatal error. Both are the gate seeing it;
