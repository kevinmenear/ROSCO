THE WRONG ARTIFACT, KEPT UNEDITED (C12).

This is the FIRST run of `run_record_form_probe.sh`, taken against the shipped
translation BEFORE anything was changed. It was written to price eighteen
candidate corpus records against the reference; it priced seventeen and found a
DEFECT IN THE TRANSLATION in the eighteenth.

    nanpay   ref iostat=0    bits=7FF8000000000000     <- gfortran: a NaN
             got iostat=5010 bits=C08EDD3B645A1CAC     <- the C++: rejected,
                                                          the item untouched

`nan(` + 58 characters + `)` is 63 characters. `parse_real`'s IEEE-word branch
copies the word into `char buf[64]` under `n < 62`, so it stops one character
short of the closing parenthesis, and `strtod` then rejects the whole field.
gfortran's own reader has no such bound.

TWO SURVIVING MUTANTS SIT ON THAT CONSTANT -- `97c42adb` (`n < 62` -> `n <= 62`)
and `4c451ef0` (`n < 62` -> `n < 63`). Both are mutants that make the
translation LESS wrong, which is why no corpus killed them and why the
`survivor_record_search` reported each as distinguished by exactly one record in
131,312: the record it found is the one this probe just failed on.

The fix, the measurement of the boundary, and the re-taken layers are in
`record_form_probe.txt` and in the evidence README. This file is kept so the
sequence is checkable: the defect was recorded before it was repaired.
