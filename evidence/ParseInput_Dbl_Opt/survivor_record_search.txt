ParseInput_Dbl_Opt -- a record that distinguishes each surviving mutant
from the SHIPPED translation, searched rather than argued.
Built with -fsanitize=address,undefined; the BASELINE ran clean under
those flags. The sanitiser is a GUARD here rather than the instrument:
byte 201 of Words is Words(2)(1:1), so a read one past the record is
inside the allocation and the difference it makes is a VALUE.

RECORD SPACE: 131312 records. Every string of length 1..3 over
  "0123456789+-.eEdDqQnNaAiIfFtTyYxX ,;/*()" at the HEAD of a 200-byte
  blank-padded record and again at its TAIL, so the token's last character
  is the record's last byte; plus 32 crafted full-width records. ONE
  element is read -- the reference transfers one -- pre-set to the
  -987.654 sentinel and printed as bits, so a stored 0.0 is
  distinguishable from an untouched slot. The record lives in the first
  200 bytes of a 400-byte object whose second half holds a parameter
  name, which is what Words(1) and Words(2) are.

WHAT A ROW MEANS. `differs` names the FIRST record on which the mutant's
(iostat, element bits) triple differs from the shipped translation's --
that record is the corpus lever and the mutant is NOT equivalent. `none`
means no record IN THIS SPACE distinguishes it, which is evidence toward
equivalence bounded by the space and is not a declaration.
