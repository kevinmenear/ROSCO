POST-INTEGRATION COPY-BACK RED TEST -- THE PREDICTION, WRITTEN BEFORE THE RUN.

The perturbation deletes ONE line from THIS unit's own wrapper in
rosco/controller/src/Controllers.f90:

    CALL vit_copy_scalars_to_localvariables(LocalVar_view, LocalVar)

and nothing else. The other copy-backs this unit's wrapper carries
(`controlparameters`, `errorvariables`) stay, and so does every other unit's
wrapper.

WHAT IT TESTS: the post-integration green is taken THROUGH the wrapper, so it
is a statement about the wrapper as much as about the translation. If the
copy-back were absent the C++ answers would never reach the Fortran
`LocalVar`, and a green that survives that is a green that was not measuring
the integration at all.

PREDICTED: 37,167 of 37,167 -- EVERY case.

THE REASONING IS A PROPERTY OF THE UNIT AND NOT OF THE CORPUS. Six
`LocalVar%...` SCALARS are assigned on EVERY path through this routine, with
no arm able to skip any of them:

    PC_MaxPit        one of the two arms of the first IF, both of which assign
    PC_MinPit        one of the two arms of the PS_Mode IF, both of which assign
    PC_KP, PC_KI, PC_KD, PC_TF   the four unconditional interp1d results
    PC_PitComT       the PIController result, then saturated, then rate-limited
    PC_PitComT_Last  assigned from it, unconditionally

All eight are in R4's compared out-parameter list. For a case to PASS with the
copy-back deleted, the incoming case value would have to coincide with the
computed one on ALL of them simultaneously.

THE ARRAYS ARE NOT WHAT THIS TESTS, and that is worth saying because they are
this unit's principal outputs. `LocalVar%PitCom`, `%PitCom_SD` and
`%PitComAct` are FIXED-SIZE `DIMENSION(3)` members of the view struct, not
ALLOCATABLE ones, so whether they cross back through
`vit_copy_scalars_to_localvariables` or through the struct itself is a
property of the generated view module rather than of this prediction. If the
failing count comes in BELOW 37,167 the first thing to read is which outputs
the survivors differ on -- a count short of the whole corpus would name cases
whose eight scalar writes are all fixed points, which is a fact about the
corpus and would be reported as such rather than explained away.

`DebugVar` and `objInst` cross by `C_LOC` with no view and are NOT affected by
this deletion.
