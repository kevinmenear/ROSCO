THE THREE ROWS THE KERNEL SCORES 0 ARE THREE DIFFERENT FACTS, and only the
harness separates them:

  no-max        0 of 62 and 192 of 1036. The max IS observable; the kernel's
                62 cases all have PRC_Min_Pitch == 0.0 and so cannot see it.
  no-errmsg     0 of 62 and 1004 of 1036. The error tail IS observable; the
                kernel's 62 cases all have aviFAIL == 0, and
                coverage/line_coverage.json says the simulation never reaches
                it either -- 0 hits in 27 scenarios. Two instruments blind,
                one not.
  max-swapped   0 of 62 AND 0 of 1036. EQUIVALENT, and now measured rather
                than argued: 1036 cases in which PRC_Min_Pitch is drawn
                independently, and exchanging the operands of a commutative
                intrinsic moves nothing.

So the claim in kernel.stubs.txt -- that a pass at `no-max` and a pass at
`max-swapped` mean different things -- is not a reading of the code. It is a
192 beside a 0.
