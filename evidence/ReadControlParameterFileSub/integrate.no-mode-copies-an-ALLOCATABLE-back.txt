`--auto-allocate` re-emits the WHOLE 193-call parse block into the wrapper,
ahead of the C++ call, wrapped in error checks over five names that do not
exist in that scope (ErrStat2, SetErrStat, ErrID_Fatal, ErrStat, ErrMsg) --
and then still copies back only scalars. It does not compile, and if it did it
would run the reference's body and the translation's body one after the other.
