RefSpeedExclusion -- the corpus partitioned by the reference's own arms
=======================================================================

Unit #59. Measured 2026-08-20 on the SCORED corpus
(`translations/ControllerBlocks/refspeedexclusion_test/refspeedexclusion_cases.bin`,
3,967 cases, the file `harness/RefSpeedExclusion.json`'s green was taken on)
by patching ONE `fprintf` into the generated `refspeedexclusion_test.cpp`
immediately before the call, running with `harness.sh --no-generate` so the
corpus was kept byte for byte, and stripping the `fprintf` afterwards.

The probe reads only the INPUTS, so which tree it links against changes the
answers and not the questions -- the RUNBOOK's unit #47 recipe.

WHY THIS EXISTS. Every stub red test below is priced as a POINT before it is
run. A prediction that is a bracket is a prediction nobody can be wrong about.

THE BAND ARM IS 55 CASES OF 3,967 -- 1.4%, AND IT IS THE THIN ONE. It is thin
for a reason that is not fixable by a pin: the band is an OPEN interval of width
`TRA_ExclBand` around `TRA_ExclSpeed`, and `VS_RefSpd`, `WE_GearboxRatio`,
`TRA_ExclSpeed` and `TRA_ExclBand` are four independent draws on the +/-1e3
default. Widening the band by pinning `TRA_ExclBand` large would DELETE the
3,912-case arm rather than add to the 55. Stated here so that a mutation
survivor landing in the band arm is read against a known 55 rather than against
an assumption.

WHAT THE PINS BOUGHT, IN ONE NUMBER: the band arm went from **55 cases of 3,967
(1.4%)** to **4,752 of 13,777 (34.5%)**. The corpus grew 3.5x and the arm this
unit exists for grew 86x, because the old corpus spent three quarters of itself
dividing by zero and another two thirds of the remainder on an inverted band.

A CROSS-INSTRUMENT CONTROL, AND IT IS FREE. `845` is the count of cases with
`restart` true AND `FA_Hist > 0`, computed here from the inputs. The mutation
sweep computed the same number twice from the OUTPUTS, on two different
statements:

    negate_cond  'if (LocalVar->FA_Hist > 0)'            killed 845 / 13777   L96
    negate_cond  'if (VS_RefSpeed_LSS > TRA_ExclSpeed)'  killed 845 / 13777   L113

Both of those sites are reachable exactly on that cell, and both agree with the
input-side count to the case. Two instruments, one number.

THE SNAP ARMS ARE STILL LOPSIDED -- 22 upper against 823 lower -- and the reason
is arithmetic rather than luck: `TRA_ExclSpeed` is 1.0 or 0.8 and the band is
2.0 or 0.1 wide, so the in-band region above the centre is `[1.0, 2.0)` at the
wide setting while the region below it reaches down to 0.0 and contains most of
`VS_RefSpd`'s ladder -- every rung below 1.0, and there are many. 22 is thin and
it is stated rather than smoothed: the `snap-down-only` stub below measures the
upper arm at exactly that, and one mutant of the sweep (`3bd34bbe`, the `+` on
the upper snap) is killed on it.
