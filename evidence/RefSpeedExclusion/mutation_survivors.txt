Every one of these needs an input at or just inside a band EDGE, and the first
corpus had none -- not because the edges are unreachable but because the corpus
was not computing anything. `inputs_census.txt`, one `fprintf` over the scored
3,967 cases:

    CntrPar%WE_GearboxRatio == 0.0    3027 of 3967   the unit's first statement
                                                     is `VS_RefSpd / that`
      of those, VS_RefSpd == 0 too    2667           -> 0.0/0.0 = NaN
    CntrPar%TRA_ExclBand    == -600   2863           -> the band is EMPTY
    the in-band arm                     55 of 3967   1.4%

`_base` puts an unstated real at `lo + (hi-lo)*frac` of the +/-1e3 default, and
for these three parameters that lands on the degenerate region. Three quarters
of the corpus divided by zero, every comparison below answered FALSE on both
sides, and the harness was GREEN throughout -- which is what a corpus that
agrees about nothing looks like.

The repair is three `values` pins in `harness/ranges.toml`, justified from the
reference's own documentation (`Gearbox ratio [>=1]`, a rotor SPEED, the SIZE of
a band) and from all 22 shipped `Examples/DISCON*.IN`, with the instrumental
half of the choice said plainly in the entry: `1.0`, `1.0` and `2.0` are picked
so that at `WE_GearboxRatio = 1` the three quantities the unit compares
`VS_RefSpd` against are `0.0`, `1.0` and `2.0` -- three rungs `VS_RefSpd`'s own
R6 ladder already contains exactly.

    in-band arm      55 of 3,967 (1.4%)  ->  4,752 of 13,777 (34.5%)
    all five mutants alive              ->  all five dead, nothing declared

WHY A WIDENING COULD NOT HAVE DONE IT. Three of the five need an exact IEEE
equality between a quotient of two free reals and a sum of two more. No range in
`harness/ranges.toml` states a relation over the reals -- `implied_by` derives a
0/1 flag from a scalar test and is the file's only relational entry -- so the
coincidence has to be CONSTRUCTED by choosing the constants, which is what the
pins do. That is the finding this unit contributes, and it is written into
DECISIONS.md as well as into the pin's own comment.

C. WHAT 1.0000 DOES NOT COVER
------------------------------

1. **The 27-scenario gate reaches this unit through ONE scenario.** `TRA_Mode`
   is 0 in 21 of the 22 shipped `Examples/DISCON*.IN`, and the call site is
   under `IF (CntrPar%TRA_Mode > 0)`, so 26 scenarios never enter the unit at
   all. Both positive gate perturbations moved only scenario 9's channels
   (30,051 and 29,895 of 5,252,000, three channels each). Whatever this unit
   does under a different tuning of the exclusion band, no gate scenario in this
   repository exercises it.

2. **The two snap-to-edge statements are DEAD IN THE PROGRAM.**
   `ControllerBlocks.f90:793` and `:795` have zero hits in all 27 scenarios: the
   one restart call scenario 9 makes arrives with `FA_Hist == 0`. The
   differential harness is the only instrument that reaches them, at 22 and 823
   cases, and `gate/RefSpeedExclusion.redtest.dead_snap_control.json` measures
   the gate's blindness to them directly -- a 1.0e30 write on :793 moved 0 of
   5,252,000.

3. **`WE_GearboxRatio`, `TRA_ExclSpeed` and `TRA_ExclBand` have left R6's
   real-magnitude ladder.** The pins that repaired the corpus took the subnormal,
   flush-to-zero and overflow rungs and negative zero off those three. A defect
   that shows only at an extreme magnitude of the DIVISOR or of the band has
   nothing here to disagree with. `VS_RefSpd` and `TRA_RateLimit` keep their full
   ladders, so the arithmetic is still swept from 5e-324 to 1e300 through the
   dividend.

4. **`WE_GearboxRatio` can no longer be zero or negative**, and the reference's
   behaviour there (NaN, or an inverted band) is now outside the tested domain by
   construction. That is a deliberate narrowing and it is the price of (A).

5. **The upper snap arm is 22 cases of 13,777.** It is the thinnest cell in the
   partition. One mutant (`3bd34bbe`) is killed on it and one stub measures it at
   exactly 22, so it is not empty -- but it is one order of magnitude thinner
   than its mirror at 823, for the arithmetic reason recorded in
   `arm_partition.txt`.

6. **One mutant of the 36 was never built under the sanitiser.** The primary
   sweep records `nocompile: 1`; the value-oracle sweep built and killed all 36.
   The union covers every mutant -- 33 killed by value, 3 declared -- but the
   PRIMARY artifact's denominator is 32 and not 33, and that is stated here
   rather than left to be inferred from two files.

7. **`--sanitize` bought nothing, and that is an answer rather than a
   disappointment.** `killed_by_sanitizer` is 0 and the survivor SET is identical
   with the sanitiser on and off. This translation contains no array subscript
   and no pointer arithmetic of its own -- the only indexing in the call chain is
   `rlP%LastSignal(inst)` inside `ratelimit`, which is unit #43's code and is the
   same implementation on both sides of every comparison here. The clean build
   did not report under `-fsanitize=address,undefined`, which is the check that
   would have been a finding.
