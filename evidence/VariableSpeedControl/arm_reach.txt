WHY THE BIG NUMBER IS `kom` AND NOT `tsr`. `harness/ranges.toml` states
`CntrPar_VS_ControlMode = { values = [1] }`, so the BASE DRAW of every non-knob
case is the K*Omega^2 mode; modes 2, 3 and 4 are reached only inside R7's
predicate-knob block, which is 512 cases of the corpus. That is a deliberate
choice and its price is exactly the 154. See `annihilation_probe.txt` for why
the base draw had to be moved at all, and for the measured generation ceiling
that stopped it being moved for `VS_FBP`, `VS_ConstPower` and `VS_State` too.

WHAT THE ZEROS ARE USED FOR, AND WHAT THEY ARE NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`st6_fbp1 = 0` and `ol2_init = 0` are the two counters that carry a declaration
in `mutation/VariableSpeedControl.unreachable.json`, and both are used ONLY for
mutants whose STATEMENT sits inside the corresponding guard -- a statement no
case executes cannot be told from any other statement there.

They are NOT used for mutants that sit ON a guard. `negate_cond` and
`compare_op` at `:287` are evaluated on all 2 `st6` cases whatever `VS_FBP`
holds, so "the body is never entered" is not a reason those mutants cannot be
observed; it is a reason to expect them to be observable and they are not. Those
stay OPEN and are named in `evidence/VariableSpeedControl/README.md` rather than
declared, because the honest word for them is "the corpus reaches this and
cannot discriminate it", and this campaign has no key for that.

Nor are `st1 = 0` and `st6 = 2` used at all: no surviving mutant's statement is
inside the Region 1.5 arm, and the Region_3_FBP arm at `:285` is entered twice,
which is not zero.
