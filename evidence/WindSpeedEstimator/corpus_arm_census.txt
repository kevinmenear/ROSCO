THE THREE THINGS THIS MEASUREMENT DECIDED

1. CORPUS 1's GREEN WAS A STATEMENT ABOUT ONE ARM. 13,868 of 13,868 passed and
   every one of them took `WE_Vw = HorWindV_F`. Both estimators -- the bulk of
   the unit -- were never entered. Nothing in the harness's own output says so:
   `checked 13868 failed 0` reads identically either way.

2. `isnan` 61 -> 0 IS `--persist-nested` DOING WHAT ITS DOCSTRING SAYS. At
   corpus 2 every one of the 61 EKF-update cases ended in the NaN repair,
   because `LocalVar%WE` is a nested type the generator zeroes, so `v_h` is 0
   and `lambda = ... / v_h` is a division by zero on the FIRST statement of the
   arm. Carrying WE across cases makes the recurrence run on the state the
   previous case left, which is the only state the shipped program ever
   presents after its first call.

3. TWO PREDICTIONS WERE REFUTED BY THEIR OWN RUNS, and both refutations named
   the next lever. `iStatus` as a RANGE moved `ekf` 61 -> 1, and `WE_Op` as a
   range moved it 1 -> 0; the same two parameters as `values` FLAGS moved it
   0 -> 1,173. A `values` list is a guarantee that every base point is re-run
   under every declared value; a `lo`/`hi` range is a draw, and two separate
   draws each moved their arm by less than one part in a thousand. That
   difference is `generate.py:2091` and it is not obvious from `ranges.toml`.

WHAT IS STILL NOT REACHED, stated rather than left to be found: `ekf` is 1,173
of 63,020, so the Jacobian and the two covariance MATMUL blocks are exercised on
under 2% of the corpus, and `isnan` is 12. Every mutation survivor whose site is
inside that arm is a survivor over 1,173 cases, not over 63,020.

CORPUS 7, TAKEN AT THE P12 RE-TAKE, AND IT IS A DIFFERENT QUESTION FROM THE
FIRST SIX
===========================================================================

Corpora 1-6 above asked WHICH ARM the corpus reaches. Corpus 7 asks what the
arm it does reach is worth, and the answer was that most of it was being
multiplied by zero.

THE QUESTION WAS ASKED BY A SURVIVOR AND NOT BY A READING.

    426e7e7e  drop_call  std::fmax(0.0, Cp_op) -> 0.0      SURVIVED

That mutant replaces the Cp clamp with the constant `0.0`. It can only survive
if the clamp already returns `0.0` on every case that evaluates it. So the
translation was re-run with a sign census
(`windspeedestimator.subscript-census.cpp`):

    corpus 6   ekf=1173  cp_raw_pos=0     cp_zero=1173   F12_nonzero=10
               cp_raw = [-935.47824179921167, -387.1384827236763]
               cpmat  = [-935.47824179921167,  943.86307337606854]

`Cp_op` was ZERO in all 1,173 EKF-update cases, and `F(1,2)`/`F(1,3)` --
which `Cp_op` multiplies entire -- were zero in 1,163 of them. (The other ten
are `om_r == 0`, where `1/om_r` is infinite and `0 * inf` is NaN. A NaN is not
an oracle either: it is the same answer on both sides of every mutant in that
product.) That one fact was the standing explanation for twenty-odd of the
sixty survivors: the whole of the Jacobian's wind-speed column had no oracle,
because every mutation of every factor in it was multiplied by zero.

TWO TAKES, AND THE FIRST WAS REFUTED BY ITS OWN RUN.

    corpus 7a  PerfData_Cp_mat = { lo = -0.1, hi = 0.5 }
               predicted  cp_raw_pos 0 -> most of 1,173
               measured   cp_raw_pos 0 -> 1,  cp_zero 1,172
               cp_raw = [-0.080643472539763505, 0.083858455182897071]

The band was right and the SIDE of it was wrong. `lambda` reaches -inf and
1e302, so interp2d clamps to a corner on essentially every case, and
`generate._fill_array` ramps monotonically from `lo` -- so the corner it clamps
to is the BOTTOM of the band. A band straddling zero puts the whole corpus just
below zero: the same annihilation, one hundredth the size.

CONFIRMED, including the part that could have failed: `ekf` is still 1,173, so
narrowing the Beta axis did not collapse the pitch saturation and empty the
estimator arms -- which is the failure mode the four entries at corpus 4 exist
to avoid, and the reason `hi = 30` rather than `hi = 25`.

THE SECOND ENTRY, and it is a different lever from the first. `Beta_vec` was
pinned to 0..90 degrees at corpus 4 so that `WE_Inp_Pitch*R2D` lands inside the
axis. It does -- but `BlPitchCMeas` is pinned to [-0.1, 0.5] RADIANS, i.e.
[-5.7, 28.6] degrees, so the top two thirds of a 0..90 axis was unreachable and
two survivors lived in the gap: `Max_Op_Pitch = Beta_vec(n)*D2R` (whose
`n-1 -> n-2` mutant moves a bound from 1.57 rad to 1.26 rad, both far above
anything `BlPitchCMeas` can be) and the `R2D` constant that positions the
lookup.
