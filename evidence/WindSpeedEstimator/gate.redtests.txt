RED TEST 1. `WE_Gamma/WE_Jtot` -> `WE_Gamma/WE_Jtot*1.000001`, the I&I arm's
gain. Clean ControllerBlocks.f90:391, reached by SCENARIO 17 AND BY NOTHING
ELSE (15,062 hits, coverage/line_coverage.json). Unit #48 measured this exact

RED TEST 2. `Ti = 0.18` -> `Ti = 0.180001`, the EKF's turbulence intensity. It
enters through `Q(2,2) = (PI * v_m**3.0 * Ti**2.0) / L` and nowhere else, so it
moves the process-noise covariance's second diagonal, hence P, hence K, hence
the state estimate, hence WE_Vw. Every scenario but 17 runs `WE_Mode = 2`, and
the gate's own first take showed that a 2-ULP difference in this chain reaches
`bld_pitch` after a few hundred timesteps. PREDICTION: this moves a LARGE number
of values -- hundreds of thousands, across most of the 26 EKF scenarios and most
of the 13 channels -- and it is the test that says the gate can see this unit's
EKF at all.

RED TEST 3. `LocalVar->WE_Op = 1` -> `LocalVar->WE_Op = 0` in the final ELSE of
the operational-range ladder. This is the PREDICATE, not the arithmetic: with
`WE_Op` never 1, both estimators are switched off and every scenario falls
through to `WE_Vw = HorWindV_F`. PREDICTION: it moves MORE than red test 2 --
it is the whole estimator rather than a coefficient inside it -- and it is the
control that separates "the gate sees the EKF's numbers" from "the gate sees
whether the EKF ran".

RED TEST 1 IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST AND IT MOVED NOTHING, EXACTLY AS
PREDICTED. `gate.py` reports it as `RED TEST FAIL` and prints unit #11's warning
about attempt 1, which is the right default and is not what happened here: the
prediction was written down as ZERO before the run, on unit #48's measurement
that `Examples/DISCON.IN:123` sets `WE_Gamma = 0.0`. Perturbing a factor that
multiplies an exact zero cannot move anything, and the reason to run it anyway
was that unit #48's two probes perturbed AeroDynTorque's return value -- upstream
of the gain -- while this one perturbs the gain itself, which is the one place
that argument could have broken. It did not.

  WHAT THIS COSTS, STATED. The entire `WE_Mode == 1` arm -- four statements,
  clean :389 to :393, 15,062 hits in scenario 17 and none anywhere else -- has
  NO GATE ORACLE. Any mutation survivor whose site is in that arm is a gate
  blind spot as well as a corpus question, and the differential harness (8,299
  cases through that arm at corpus 6) is the only instrument that reaches it.

RED TESTS 2 AND 3 ARE A PAIR, AND THE ORDER OF THEIR COUNTS IS THE POINT.
Perturbing a COEFFICIENT inside the EKF moved 1,084,204; switching the whole
estimator OFF moved 1,486,321. The second is the larger, which is what a control
should show: the gate sees both that the EKF ran and what its numbers were, and
the difference between the two counts is the part of the output that depends on
the estimator's VALUE rather than on its PRESENCE.
