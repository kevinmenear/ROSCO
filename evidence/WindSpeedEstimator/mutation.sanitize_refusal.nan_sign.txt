THE SANITISED BASELINE'S ONE FAILING CASE IS A NaN'S SIGN BIT, AND IT IS NOT
THE SANITISER'S DOING

Follow-on to `mutation.sanitize_refusal.txt`, which recorded the refusal and
left the cause as a stated hypothesis ("the likeliest reading is a NaN or a
signed zero whose bit pattern the two codegens spell differently") together with
the reason it could not be tested: the emitted mismatch record truncates a
nested type at 32 bytes and `we_t` is nineteen doubles.

IT IS NOW MEASURED, AND THE HYPOTHESIS WAS RIGHT.

WHAT WAS RUN. Two changes, both local and both reverted afterwards:

  1. `hexbytes()`'s `size_t lim = n < 32 ? n : 32` in the GENERATED
     `windspeedestimator_test.cpp` widened to 256, so a 152-byte nested type
     prints whole. The generator is `translation-loop@8e04515
     harness/emit.py:1238`; it was NOT edited, because editing it moves
     `loop_rev` and this unit's artifacts would then name two instruments
     (X3, and `revcheck` refuses a split). The 32-byte cap is raised as a
     finding instead.
  2. The translation replaced by `windspeedestimator.subscript-census.cpp` --
     the SAME source with a bounds-recording wrapper on five array
     declarations and a handful of counters. It changes no arithmetic. It
     changes inlining and stack layout, which is the whole point: it is a
     codegen perturbation that is not the sanitiser.

(bytes are little-endian, so `000000000000f8ff` is the double
`0xfff8000000000000` and `000000000000f87f` is `0x7ff8000000000000`.)

FIFTEEN QUIET NaNs ON EACH SIDE. FOURTEEN OF THEM DIFFER, AND EVERY DIFFERENCE
IS EXACTLY ONE BIT: THE SIGN. Both sides are `NaN`. Neither is more correct
than the other. IEEE-754 does not specify the sign of a NaN produced by an
invalid operation, and gfortran and g++ -- and g++ at one inlining decision
versus another -- are each entitled to whichever they emit.

The fifteenth, `xh(2,1)`, agrees, which is the control the table carries in
itself: if the difference were an ordinary value disagreement it would not stop
at a sign bit on fourteen fields and skip the fifteenth.

WHY THE SHIPPED TRANSLATION IS GREEN AND EVERY PERTURBED BUILD IS NOT. The
shipped build happens to emit `fff8...` at all fifteen, which is what gfortran
emits, so `memcmp` succeeds. That agreement is a coincidence of codegen and
nothing in the translation asks for it. Add a counter, turn on
`-fsanitize=address,undefined`, or mutate a line, and some of the fifteen flip.

WHAT THIS INVALIDATES, STATED PLAINLY.

  * THE SANITISED SWEEP IS BLOCKED BY THE INSTRUMENT AND NOT BY THE
    TRANSLATION. `vit_mutate.py --sanitize` refuses to score against a
    non-green baseline, correctly; the baseline is non-green because the
    oracle is comparing a bit IEEE-754 leaves free. 35 of this unit's
    survivors are the index/bound class that only `--sanitize` reaches, and
    they stay unreachable until the comparison stops adjudicating NaN signs.

  * IT REACHES THE UNSANITISED SWEEP TOO, IN THE OTHER DIRECTION. `LocalVar%WE`
    is UNOBSERVABLE-but-compared: the generator does not expand it into
    parameters, so it is zero-initialised, not varied, and memcmp'd after the
    call. Twelve of the 63,020 cases leave a NaN in it. On those twelve cases a
    mutant that changes nothing but codegen can be scored KILLED, and a mutant
    that changes a value can be scored killed for the wrong reason. Nothing in
    `mutation/WindSpeedEstimator.json` distinguishes the two.

  * IT IS NOT SPECIFIC TO THIS UNIT. Any unit whose corpus can reach an invalid
    operation and whose outputs include a bytewise-compared nested type has it.
    `LocalVar` alone carries five.

BOTH ARE RAISED IN `.loop-run/findings.jsonl` -- the NaN-sign comparison as an
`invalidating_finding`, the 32-byte truncation as a `default_change_proposed`
(it cost this campaign one dispatch's worth of "cannot be localised further",
and the fix is one integer).

WHAT WAS NOT DONE. The comparison was not changed. Making `memcmp` NaN-aware is
a change to a verification default in the middle of a run (X3) and it is the
Driver's to make, not this unit's. The twelve NaN cases were not excluded
either: excluding them is a corpus judgement that needs the same decision.
