WHAT THIS CENSUS SAYS, and none of it is "the translation is wrong"

1. 36 OF THE 60 ARE IN ARM F, which the corpus enters 1,173 times. That arm went
   from 0 cases to 1,173 during this dispatch, on six `ranges.toml` entries and
   `--persist-nested`; the next factor of five is the same kind of work, and it
   is priced rather than done. The binding condition is now `RestartWSE != 0`,
   which needs `iStatus != 0` AND neither `WE_Op` transition to fire -- and the
   second is a relation between a DRAWN flag and a COMPUTED one, which is the
   shape `ranges.toml` has no key for.

2. 11 ARE `compare_op`, AND MOST SIT ON LOOP BOUNDS THE TRANSLATION WROTE ITSELF
   (`i < 3`, `k < 9`, `k < 3`). Widening one by a rung reads or writes past the
   end of a 3- or 9-element automatic array: the difference is at addresses the
   program does not own, and a VALUE comparison cannot see that class at all --
   three identical runs over one such mutant scored 0.983, 1.000, 0.983 in this
   campaign, and the 1.000 is the run that measured least.
   **`vit_mutate.py --sanitize` is the instrument for these and it was NOT run.**
   That is the single largest remaining item and it is one sweep.

3. 10 ARE `index_offset` OR `collapse_stride` AND 15 ARE `drop_factor`, largely on the
   Jacobian's own flat index arithmetic (`F[1 * 3 + 0]`, `[k]`, `[9]`). Same
   address class as (2). The flat-array spelling was chosen over `double F[3][3]`
   so the column-major arithmetic stays visible to a reader and to the sweep; it
   also created these sites, and that trade is stated here rather than defended.

4. 4 ARE `assoc_reorder` ON GROUPINGS THE REFERENCE ITSELF WROTE --
   `PI * (R*R)` -> `(PI*R)*R` and `Cp_op * (v_h*v_h)` -> `(Cp_op*v_h)*v_h`.
   Unit #48 KILLED the first of these one function over, 52 cases of 1,131, so
   the shape is killable and this corpus's magnitudes simply do not separate
   them. Cheapest of the four to attack: the operands are `WE_BladeRadius`,
   `Cp_op` and `v_h`, and the first is already pinned in `ranges.toml`.

5. ARM C HAS ZERO SURVIVORS, WHICH IS THE ONE PLEASANT SURPRISE HERE. The I&I
   filter is the arm the gate cannot observe at all -- `WE_Gamma = 0.0`
   annihilates it, 0 of 5,252,000 -- so a survivor there would have had one
   instrument. Every mutant in it was killed by the differential harness's 8,299
   cases through that arm. The blind spot is real and it cost nothing in this
   sweep; both halves of that are worth saying, because the arm is four
   statements and the next translation with a dead gain may not be.
