  Twelve bytes for three default LOGICALs is FOUR BYTES EACH, and .TRUE. is

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE. It answers "is the translation right about
these three Fortran semantics" -- yes, at 12 bytes and 16 records, red-tested
three ways. It does NOT make the CORPUS reach them: `mutation/
WriteRestartFile.unreachable.json` still declares 7cb6d9d1, 818543b1 and
f3829a51 unreachable, and that declaration is still true. The two statements are
about different things and both are worth having; the declaration says the sweep
cannot discriminate, this says the discrimination would go the right way.
