## what the numbers mean

Unit #41's rule: a red result that is really an undefined reference cannot be
told from a translation defect, and `vit_mutate.py` refuses to score against a
red baseline, so nothing downstream could close either.

Two rules produce the descending bodies, and it took two measurements to find
the second:

    order ladder excluded only     1117 -> 1029 cases, 5 failures -> 4
    R10's reversed body as well    1029 -> 1005 cases, 4 failures -> 0

So R6's ordering sweep accounted for ONE of the five and R10's reduction
crossing -- `xq` set to `nextafter(MINVAL(xData), +inf)` over the body and its
REVERSE -- for the other four. `_fill_array` already draws strictly ascending
bodies, so those two rules are the only things in the generator that leave the
reference's own domain. `harness/ranges.toml`'s `[interp2d]` states both
exclusions and what each costs.
